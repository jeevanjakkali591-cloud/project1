How the 8-bit Circuit Works

The circuit takes two 8-bit binary inputs, A and B. Each input bit is combined using AND logic to generate partial products. The partial products are then reduced using approximate compressor logic. Finally, half adders, full adders and a reduction adder combine the remaining sum and carry signals to produce the approximate multiplication result.

01 — INPUT A[7:0] and B[7:0] enter the multiplier.
02 — AND GATES Input bits generate the partial products.
03 — APPROXIMATION Approximate compressors reduce hardware complexity and introduce controlled error.
04 — OUTPUT Adders combine the signals and produce the approximate multiplication result.
